ClearDue — B2B Collections Agent

checking…

ClearDue chases overdue B2B invoices — negotiates settlements, respects hard limits a human set, and closes with a payment link. Guardrails are declared, not hand-rolled: every tool call passes through policy_engine, a reusable module of composable rules (cumulative discount caps, consent checks, escalation thresholds) that any tool can declare against. An autonomous LLM debtor — not a script — found a real gap this way: three separate payment links, each individually fine, summing to ₹90,000 against a ₹45,000 invoice. Fixed and reverified. Try the presets below, then check the Policy Audit tab to see every decision, not just what succeeded.
